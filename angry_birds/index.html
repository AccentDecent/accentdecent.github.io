What you can build

Accurate physics

Matter.js rigid bodies. Damage scales with impact impulse (speed × weight), so heavy/fast hits do more — like the original.

Every block type

Wood, stone, ice, glass, sand, steel, gold, TNT, rubber, balloons — each with hardness, weight, friction, bounce. Invent your own.

Custom birds

Built-in powers (split, bomb, boomerang, freeze…) or write a brand-new ability in a few lines of script.

Scripted bosses

Multi-phase bosses with HP thresholds, built-in AI behaviors, custom logic, and automatic camera moves.

Camera & cutscenes

Follow, focus, zoom, shake, and full keyframed cutscene sequences — all controllable from scripts.

Texture overrides

Drop in your own art per block, bird, or pig — stored inline so your game stays a portable static file.

Multi-level stories

Chain levels into a campaign with story cards, star ratings, level select, and saved progress.

Ship anywhere

Export a game.json or a single self-contained HTML. Host on any static site — the player needs no editor.
